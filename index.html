Codes & Definitions
RO — Routine out opportunity converted.
MRO-F — Missed routine out: fielding.
MRO-D — Missed routine out: decision or hesitation.
MRO-T — Missed routine out: throw.
MRO-R — Missed routine out: range or positioning.
MRO-1B — Missed routine out: first-base reception.
XA — Extra advance allowed.
COV — Coverage, backup, or cutoff mistake.
PLUS — Excellent or above-routine play.
Log Event
A missed-out button records one RO plus exactly one primary MRO cause. XA, COV, and PLUS do not add an RO.
